Pricing questions

How does the free trial work?

You can process up to 50 pages and create one report during the trial. The allowance is a one-time total, and you can start without a payment card.

When does a page count?

A page uses your allowance when you confirm you want to extract information from it. You can upload a file and review the detected page count before starting.

When does a report count?

Creating a new full or focused report uses one report from your allowance. Reopening or sharing a report you already created uses no additional allowance.

Does work in my doctor’s account use my allowance?

Your personal allowance covers the pages you process and reports you create from your own account. Work your doctor does in their account uses their allowance.

Which plan fits my records?

Estimate how many pages you want to add, including older records, and how often you want a new report. For help estimating, contact the team.
