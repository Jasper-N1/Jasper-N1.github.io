Questions

Frequently asked questions

01What is a digital twin?

A living model of your health, built from everything you share with us: lab results, scans, wearable data, doctor’s letters and how you live. It brings it all into one picture and keeps itself up to date as new data comes in.

02What can I upload or connect?

Lab reports and blood tests (PDFs or photos), scans and imaging reports, letters from your doctors, and data from your watch, ring or phone. If you’re not sure, upload it and we’ll work out what it is.

03How does the clinical intelligence work?

It reads each record, pulls out the results that matter, matches them to the right measures and checks them against published medical guidelines. Every finding links back to the record it came from, so you can see where it came from.

04Is my health data private and secure?

Your data is encrypted, never sold, and only shared when you choose to share it. You can export or delete it at any time.

05Does it replace my doctor?

No. Your twin helps you understand your health and prepares you for better conversations with your doctor. It doesn’t diagnose or prescribe.

06Can I share my twin with my doctor or family?

Yes. Share a full report or just one part of it, choose exactly who sees what, and stop sharing whenever you like.

07How much does it cost?

You can start for free and see your twin before you pay for anything. Pricing details are coming soon.
