Privacy

Your health data. Your choices.

From the records you add to the reports you share, here’s what happens to your information and how to manage it.

Read the full Privacy Notice Visit the N1 Trust Centre ↗

We protect your records

Health records are encrypted during transfer and storage. Access requires an authorized account or a sharing link for the report you choose.

Health data stays out of ads

We don’t sell patient data, give it to data brokers, or use it for targeted advertising.

AI helps you read your records

AI can organize your records and draft reports. Our AI providers receive the information needed for the task. Neither we nor those providers use patient data to train general-purpose models.

What you can control

These controls apply to your personal account. If a clinic manages your record, contact them about access or changes.

What you add
Choose which records to upload and which services to connect.
Who can see a report
Share an individual report when you’re ready. You can password-protect the link, set an expiry, or turn it off.
What you take with you
Export your available records or download a report to keep a copy.
What you remove
Delete individual files or request account deletion from Settings. Some copies may remain temporarily in backups, and some information may need to be kept for legal or security reasons.

Common privacy questions

What information do you collect?

We collect what you provide, such as account details, uploaded records, and support messages. We also collect technical information, such as device and sign-in activity, to run and secure the service.

Can my doctor see my uploads?

Uploads to your personal account aren’t automatically visible to your doctor. You can choose to share a report. If a clinic created or manages the record, its access rules apply.

Where is my information stored?

The Trust Centre lists where uploaded files, databases, and logs are hosted and explains the difference between them.

What if I connect an outside AI assistant?

Connecting an assistant allows it to request information within the access you grant. The assistant’s provider handles what it receives under its own privacy terms. You can disconnect it in the assistant’s settings. See the Privacy Notice for details.

Questions about your data?

Contact N1 to ask about privacy or request access, a correction, or deletion. Please keep lab reports and other health records out of ordinary email.
