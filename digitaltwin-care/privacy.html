Privacy

Your health data stays yours.

This page is the short version. The Privacy Notice is the full legal text, and the Trust Centre has the security details.

Read the Privacy Notice Visit the Trust Centre ↗

Not for sale

We don’t sell your health data, and we don’t use it for ads.

Not for training

We don’t use your health data to train AI models.

You choose who sees it

Doctors and other people see your records only when you connect with them or share.

We never

  • Sell patient data
  • Use it for targeted ads
  • Give it to data brokers
  • Use it to train AI models
  • Use it for research without written permission

Who sees your data

Only you, the people you choose, and the few services that run N1.

Doctors you connect with
A doctor sees your records, results, and reports only after you connect. Disconnect a doctor at any time, and their access stops at once.
People you share a report with
You share one report through a link. Anyone who saved a copy keeps it after the link stops working.
Your clinic
If a clinic manages your record, the clinic sets the access rules. Send requests about that record to the clinic.
N1 staff
A small group, including our team in Malaysia, can open records for support, maintenance, and incident response. They do this only inside N1’s systems.
AI providers
OpenAI and Google (Vertex AI) read your records to answer your questions and write reports. Both work for N1 under a business associate agreement (BAA). N1 doesn’t keep the text of AI requests in its logs.
AI assistants you connect
If you connect an outside assistant, it gets only what you allow when you connect it. Its provider’s privacy terms apply to that data. Disconnect it in the assistant’s settings.

Your controls

Share, take, or delete your data yourself, in the app.

Share a report Report → Share
Add a password, set the link to expire after 1 to 90 days, see how many times it was opened, or turn it off.
Export your data Settings → Export data
Download your results, diagnoses, procedures, medications, supplements, and records.
Delete a file
Delete any uploaded file at any time.
Delete your account Settings → Delete my account
Sign-in stops at once, and connected doctors lose access. N1 then deletes your files in every region, your database records, and your sign-in identity. Encrypted backups clear on their normal cycle, and some legal or security records may be kept.
Correct a record
Contact N1, or your clinic if it manages your record.

Where your data lives

Encrypted, never public, and in a region you choose.

Your files
You choose a region when you register: the US, the EU (Frankfurt), the UK (London), Singapore, India (Mumbai), or Australia (Sydney). You can’t change it later.
Your records and logs
Stored in the US for every account.
Protection
Encrypted while it travels and while it’s stored. Storage is never publicly accessible.
The companies that run N1
Google Cloud hosts the platform. OpenAI and Google Vertex AI run the AI features. Cloudflare protects network traffic and doesn’t store its content. Stripe handles billing and gets no health data. See the Subprocessor Register

The rules we follow

N1 Research, LLC is responsible for your data.

If your clinic manages your record
N1 works for the clinic under a HIPAA business associate agreement (BAA) in the US, or a data processing addendum (DPA) under GDPR in the EU, UK, and Switzerland.
If you signed up yourself
N1 follows the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule. If a breach affects your data, we tell you within 60 days of finding it.

Questions about your data?

Ask N1 for access, a correction, or deletion. Don’t send lab reports or other health records by email.
